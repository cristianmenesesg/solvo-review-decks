This is a short walkthrough of the automation that watches your clients’ job postings. In five minutes: what it does, what reaches your inbox, and where to find everything in Solvo Platform.

HOW IT WORKS

From your client’s job post to your inbox

1

It checks your clients

Clients are split across the weekdays, so each one is checked once a week on Indeed and LinkedIn.

2

It reads every new posting

AI decides whether Solvo can fill the role remotely and pulls out the salary, work modality, seniority and languages.

3

It researches the client once

The first time a client has new postings, it adds the website, industry, size, revenue and what the company does.

4

It tells you

You get one email per client with its new postings, and everything is saved in Solvo Platform.

Current Client Openings

Four steps, all automatic. Each weekday the system takes the clients assigned to that day, so every client is checked once a week on Indeed and LinkedIn. Every new posting is read by AI and kept only if it passes three checks: a support role Solvo covers, work that can be done remotely (even if posted as on-site), and no US state license required. The first time a client has new postings, the system researches the company once. Clients are not scored and no contacts are searched for them. The last step is the email, and everything stays in Solvo Platform.

GOOD TO KNOW

Four things to keep in mind

You only see your clients

Solvo Platform shows the companies and positions where you are the assigned Sales Rep.

Remove “Has decision makers”

Contacts aren’t searched for clients, so that default filter hides their positions.

Not viable is hidden by default

Switch Remote viability to Both to see everything your client posted.

The email covers the last 24 hours

Everything detected before that stays in Solvo Platform.

Current Client Openings

To close, four practical points. You see only the clients assigned to you. When you open Open Positions, remove “Has decision makers” or your clients’ positions won’t show. The list starts with viable positions only; switch to Both if you want to see everything a client posted. And the email is a daily summary of what’s new: the full history is always in Solvo Platform.
