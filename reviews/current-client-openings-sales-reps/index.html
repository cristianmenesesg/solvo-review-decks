Overview of Current Client Openings for Sales Reps: how it works, where client positions appear in Solvo Platform and what the email notification contains.

HOW IT WORKS

Detection, analysis and notification of client job postings

1

Weekly check of each client

Clients are distributed across the weekdays, so each one is checked once a week on Indeed and LinkedIn.

2

Analysis of each new posting

AI determines whether Solvo can fill the role remotely and identifies the salary, work modality, seniority and languages.

3

Notification to the Sales Rep

One email per client with its new viable postings. All positions remain available in Solvo Platform.

Current Client Openings

Three automatic stages. Clients are distributed across the weekdays, so each client is checked once a week on Indeed and LinkedIn. Each new posting is analyzed by AI and considered viable when it meets three criteria: a support role Solvo covers, work that can be done remotely (even when posted as on-site), and no US state license required. The assigned Sales Rep receives one email per client with the new viable postings, and every position remains available in Solvo Platform.

CONSIDERATIONS

Considerations for using Solvo Platform

Visibility by assignment

Solvo Platform shows only the companies and positions where the user is the assigned Sales Rep.

Decision makers filter

Open Positions starts on “Has decision makers”. Contacts are not searched for clients, so this filter hides their positions.

Remote viability filter

The list starts with viable positions only. The “Both” option shows every position the client has posted.

Email coverage

The email lists postings from the last 24 hours. Earlier positions remain in Solvo Platform.
